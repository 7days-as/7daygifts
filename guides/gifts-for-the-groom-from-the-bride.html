Tips

  • Think about what he already uses every day, then find a nicer version of it.
  • Add his initials or your wedding date where you can. A few letters make an everyday gift feel personal.
  • Order personalized items first and check each listing's delivery estimate.
  • A short note from you with each gift goes a long way.

Questions people ask

What is a good gift for the groom from the bride?

Something he will use often and that feels personal: a monogrammed wallet, an engraved watch box, a leather AirPods case with his initials, or a weekender bag for the trip after the wedding.

How much should the bride spend on a gift for the groom?

Whatever feels comfortable. Many brides give a few smaller gifts in the Under $25 and $25–$50 ranges and one bigger gift. Every gift on this page shows its budget range.

When does the bride give the groom a gift?

Many brides give a gift in the week before the wedding, and some give a small gift on each of the 7 days before it. Our 7-day plan has an idea for each day.
