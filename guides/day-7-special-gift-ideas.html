Tips

  • Order early. Bigger gifts can sell out, so check each listing's delivery date before you count on it.
  • Pair it with a handwritten note. On the last day, the note is often the part they keep.
  • Not sure about the style? Choose something with easy returns and tuck the gift receipt in the box.
  • Special does not have to mean expensive. A personalized keepsake with your photos or your wedding date can mean just as much.

Questions people ask

What do you give on Day 7?

Usually the most meaningful or the biggest gift of the week: a photo frame, a jewelry case, a watch, a fragrance or good headphones. Some people give it on the wedding day itself instead.

How much should the Day 7 gift cost?

There is no set amount. Many people spend a little more on the last gift than on the other six. The ideas on this page are in the $100–$200 and $200+ ranges, and a personalized keepsake can be just as special.

Can the Day 7 gift be practical?

Yes. A weekender bag or a pair of headphones gets used for years, and it reminds them of you every time.
