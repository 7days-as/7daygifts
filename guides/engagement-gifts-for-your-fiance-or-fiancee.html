Tips

  • Buying a watch or jewelry? Find out the size and style first, quietly, from a sibling or a close friend.
  • Engraving your engagement date makes even a small gift a keepsake.
  • Save the biggest gift for the wedding week. Our Day 7 page has bigger ideas.

Questions people ask

What is a good engagement gift for my fiancé?

A watch, an engraved watch box, a monogrammed wallet or an engraved pen: gifts he will use every day, especially with your engagement date on them.

What is a good engagement gift for my fiancée?

A jewelry case for her new pieces, a digital photo frame, an Open When letter box, or a fragrance she loves.

How much should an engagement gift cost?

There is no rule. The ideas here range from Under $25 to $200+, and every gift shows its budget range.
