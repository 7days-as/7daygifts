Hanukkah Gifts: Gift Ideas for Every Night of Chanukah

Hanukkah 2026 begins at sundown on Friday, December 4 and lasts eight nights, ending on Saturday, December 12. Many families give a gift on one night, and some give a small gift on each of the eight nights, so it helps to plan a mix of bigger and smaller ideas.

These are gifts to give, not decorations: the gadgets, cameras, speakers, sport tech and personalized keepsakes people are genuinely happy to open. Every gift links to its own page with a photo, a short description and a price band, from small gifts under $25 to a bigger gift for the night you want to make special.

Not sure where to start? Think about who you are shopping for first. A spouse might get the bigger gift of the week, kids and teens love cameras and speakers, and parents and grandparents often appreciate a personalized keepsake with names or a family photo. The sections below follow that order, and the last one is all small gifts that work well for the other nights.

Tips for Hanukkah gift shopping

  • Order early. Hanukkah 2026 starts on December 4, and shipping gets slower the closer you get to the busy December season.
  • Personalized gifts take the longest. Order anything engraved or printed with a name at least two to three weeks ahead.
  • If you give a gift each night, plan the list together: a few small gifts, one or two medium ones, and one bigger gift for a night you want to make special.
  • Wrap and label each gift by night ahead of time, so the week stays calm.

Questions people ask

When is Hanukkah 2026?

Hanukkah 2026 begins at sundown on Friday, December 4, 2026, when the first candle is lit, and ends at nightfall on Saturday, December 12, 2026.

What should I get my husband for Hanukkah?

Go for something he will use every day: noise-cancelling headphones, a GPS watch if he runs, a pocket camera, or an engraved watch box.

What should I get my wife for Hanukkah?

A Kindle, a temperature-control smart mug, an instant camera or personalized velvet jewelry rolls are all gifts she is likely to use and love.

What are good Hanukkah gifts for teenagers?

Teens tend to love instant cameras, portable speakers, a phone gimbal for videos and AirPods. For a smaller night, an AirTag or rechargeable hand warmers work well.

Do you give a gift every night of Hanukkah?

Some families give a gift on each of the eight nights, and others give one gift for the holiday. If you give every night, mix small gifts with one or two bigger ones.
