What she remembers is that you noticed: the scent she loves, the jewelry that has no proper home, the photos still sitting on your phone. The ideas below are built around that, with options for a first anniversary, a tenth, or anything in between.

We grouped them into jewelry and keepsake boxes, perfume, photo and memory gifts, cozy everyday things, and gifts for the two of you. Each one links to its own page with a photo, what makes it a good gift, and a price band, so it is easy to stay within your budget.

How to choose an anniversary gift for her

  • Listen for hints in the weeks before. Something she mentioned wanting, or a thing that broke, is often the best gift idea you will get.
  • Personalized items, portraits and photo gifts take time to make. Order at least two weeks ahead and check each listing's delivery estimate.
  • If you are unsure about size or style, choose a gift that does not need either: perfume in a scent she already likes, a jewelry case, or a photo gift.
  • Write a card. A few honest lines about the year you just had together is the part she is most likely to keep.

Questions people ask

What should I get my wife for our anniversary?

Choose something that shows you noticed what she loves: a perfume in her favorite scent family, a leather jewelry case, or a photo gift like a digital frame filled with pictures from your year together. Add a handwritten card.

What is a small, sweet anniversary gift for my wife?

Keep it thoughtful and personal without going big: an “Open When” letter box, a personalized velvet jewelry roll, cozy slippers or an illustrated portrait of the two of you from a favorite photo.

What do you get a woman who has everything for an anniversary?

Give an experience or a memory rather than more things: a picnic set for a day out, a scrapbook of your trips, or a crystal photo of a moment you both love. An upgrade of something she uses daily also works well.

What is a good first anniversary gift for her?

The well-known first-anniversary theme is paper, so letters, a photo book, a scrapbook or an illustrated portrait print all fit. See our first anniversary gift ideas for more.

How far ahead should I order an anniversary gift?

For personalized or engraved gifts, two to three weeks is safe. Many ready-made gifts ship in a few days, but check the delivery estimate on the listing before you count on it.
