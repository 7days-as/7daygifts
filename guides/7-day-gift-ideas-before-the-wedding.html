7 Day Gifts Before the Wedding: Ideas for Each Day

In many families, the chosson and kallah send each other a small gift every day of the week before the wedding, the days when they traditionally don't see each other. It is a sweet way to stay connected and build excitement, but planning seven gifts on top of everything else can feel like a lot.

Customs vary by family and community, so follow yours. If you want a starting point, here is one easy way to plan the week: a different theme for each day, so the gifts feel varied without overthinking it.

Tips

  • Order personalized and engraved items first. Custom gifts take longer to make and ship, so check each listing's delivery estimate on Amazon before you count on it.
  • Plan who will deliver each gift. A sibling, friend or parent often brings it over, so have everything wrapped and labeled by day.
  • A short handwritten note with each gift often means more than the gift itself.
  • Mix price points. A few small gifts and one bigger one usually feels more balanced than seven expensive ones.
